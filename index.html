THE FIRST DAY

Spawn was on a tiny island surrounded by water. The island was peaceful, but isolated.

So the four players worked together and built a bridge around 20 blocks long and 2 blocks wide to reach the mainland.

That bridge became our first great achievement — a simple path that turned a lonely spawn into the beginning of a world.

Every adventure after that started with four players stepping across those blocks.

📖 The Chronicles of Deadliner

In a world of endless oceans, four adventurers awoke upon a tiny island — a green patch surrounded by blue, with no road forward except the one they could build themselves.

They gathered wood, shaped stone, and laid a bridge across the water. Twenty blocks long and two blocks wide, it was not a grand monument by any kingdom's measure. Yet to them, it was the first road in a world that had none.

From that road came houses, farms, caves, iron, diamonds, and a village waiting beyond the horizon. Every expedition pushed the borders of their map a little farther.

Then came the stronghold: an ancient promise beneath the earth. The End Portal was found, silent and waiting.

Beyond another portal lies the Nether. Beyond the End lies a dragon. Neither has been faced yet.

The story is unfinished — and that is exactly what makes it an adventure.
